# 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Financial Performance Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** to analyze key business metrics including revenue, cost, profit, profitability, regional performance, and product-level insights.

The dashboard converts raw sales and financial data into meaningful business intelligence through interactive visualizations, helping organizations monitor performance, identify opportunities, and support strategic decision-making.

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions:

- What is the total revenue generated?
- How much total profit has been earned?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- Which region contributes the highest revenue?
- Which product category generates the highest profit?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- How does revenue fluctuate over time?
- How can profitability be improved through product and regional insights?

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 📊 Page 1: Financial Overview

This page provides a high-level summary of overall business performance.

### KPI Cards

- 💰 Total Revenue
- 💵 Total Cost
- 📈 Total Profit
- 🎯 Profit Margin %

### Visualizations

#### 📈 Total Revenue by Day
Tracks daily revenue performance and identifies business trends.

#### 🥧 Total Profit by Product Category
Shows profit contribution from Electronics, Furniture, and Office Supplies.

#### 🌍 Total Revenue by Region
Compares revenue generated across North, South, East, and West regions.

#### 📊 Product Category Revenue and Profit Analysis
Analyzes the relationship between revenue and profitability across product categories.

### Slicers

- Region
- Product Category
- Product

---

## 📦 Page 2: Product Performance Analysis

This page focuses on detailed product and category performance.

### Visualizations

#### 📊 Total Revenue by Product
Highlights top-performing products based on revenue contribution.

#### 📈 Profit by Product Category and Quantity
Analyzes profit generation relative to quantity sold.

#### 🥧 Total Revenue by Product Category
Shows category-wise revenue contribution.

#### 📉 Revenue Trend Analysis
Tracks daily revenue fluctuations and business performance patterns.

### Slicers

- Region
- Product Category
- Product
